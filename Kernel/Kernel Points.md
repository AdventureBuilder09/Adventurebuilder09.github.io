# Overview

The goal is to make a true kitchen-table format with a minimal banlist that allows for nearly unlimited deckbuilding freedom, while keeping a balanced format.

In that light, Un-cards are legal! Good luck finding synergistic ones without any common deckbuilding sites!

Similarly, Unknown Event cards, Heroes of the Realm cards, Digital Only Cards (including PAST cards, which scryfall can handle; also [EDH Silver](https://edhsilver.com/cards/uo/ast/faerie-dragon/)), and others are also legal.

Unfortunately this is limited to *only* the cards on scryfall. This is basically everything except unglued 2 the obligatory sequel and inQuest cutout cards.

The primary deckbuilding rule is Points. The base deck size (without Advantageous Proclamation) is 60, but there is no limit. You get 1 point to spend for every card in your deck (so you can run at most 4 advantageous proclamations not counting quests), plus more by having your deck fulfill certain restrictions (See [Quests](#Quests)), and your deck is legal as long as the number of points you have is more than the total point cost of your deck (See [Point Values](#Point%20Values)).

I must give a big thanks to the [Home of the UnCommander Variant - EDH Silver](https://edhsilver.com/). They are a great resource for weird, wacky, and wonderful un-heard (of) cards, and I did take a little (not a lot) of inspiration for rules when making this format

## Speed goals

Combo decks make attempts T2-3, Aggro threatens game shortly afterwards. 
<a id="Point Values"></a>
# Point values

1 card in your maindeck gives you 1 point to spend (I.E. you could have a commander creature in a 60-card deck with no other point-costing cards)

60 Card Base Min (See Advantageous Proclamation)

15 Card Sideboard

- cards in SB count for points

- 2pts per additional SB card


Playing more than the singleton value of a card (1 for most cards, 9 for nazgul, unlimited for basic lands and persistent petitioners) - 1pt for each other copy (plus any additional costs)

So to run 4 of a card, that would cost (0+1+2+3)=6pts, but to run 5 that would cost 10 pts, 15pts for 6, ect.


<a id="Command Zone"></a>
## Command Zone

Having constant access to one or more cards/abilities is very powerful \**cough* ***Lurrus*** *cough*\*. Therefore cards that you always have access to are very expensive. I did try to give good rebates with quests though.

You may have a total of one Commander (or legal partner pairing), with up to one signature spell, OR one vanguard, and up to three conspiracies.
<a id="Commanders"></a>
### Commanders

The commander will take the value of the first criteria from among the following:

Commander Land - 105

Commander Legendary Creature/Planeswalker or rulebreaker equivalent (I.E. Shorikai, partner pairings) - 80

Commander Enchantment/Nonlegendary Creature - 85

Commander Instant/Sorcery - 90

Commander Artifact CMC >1 - 95

Commander Artifact - 100

Signature Spell (Cast only while you control your commander **permanent**) - 30
- You may not have a signature spell if you have a commander instant/sorcery (including Ashaya's Enduring Bond)


<a id="Vanguards"></a>
### Vanguards

The [vanguards](https://mtg.fandom.com/wiki/Vanguard_(card_type)) range wildly in power, and I have not yet finished balancing all of them.

You may have at most 1 vanguard.

The point value for each vanguard follows the following rules:
- Base value of 60 points
- If it increases maximum hand size, add points equal to 4 times the increase
- If it increases starting life total, add points equal to the increase
- If it is any of the following, add the corresponding value:
	- [Squee](https://scryfall.com/card/pvan/107/squee) - 5 pts
	- [Ertai](https://scryfall.com/card/pvan/101/ertai) - 10 pts
	- [Elvish Champion](https://scryfall.com/card/pmoa/37/elvish-champion-avatar) - -20 (so a total value of 40)
	- [Hanna](https://scryfall.com/card/pvan/204/hanna) - 10 pts

<a id="Quests"></a>
# Quests

You can also make your deck better by fulfilling certain restrictions. Yes this is a very original idea that I came up independently of the companion mechanic.

### Singleton (20 pts)

You earn the singleton reward by spending no points on duplicate cards. You can still play 6 [Seven Dwarves](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/141/seven-dwarves) if you want. 

## Color Locked (10pts)
Exclusively if you have a commander permanent. You are Color Locked if every card in your starting deck is within your commanders(s) total color identity

These can be fun, but you must check with your opponent ***before the game starts*** that they are OK with playing and resolving these cards. Essentially, banned but suggested to be legal via rule 0.

<a id="Rulings"></a>
# Rulings
<a id="Elminster"></a>
[Elminster](https://edhsilver.com/cards/uo/inq/elminster/) (the Advanced Dungeons & Dragons – InQuest #41 one) has an ability similar to that of [Trampled Lotus](https://scryfall.com/card/unk/RA12b/trampled-lotus). You choose the card face down while activating the ability, but do not reveal it until the ability resolves (which is usually immediately).

## Destroying cards

If a card would be destroyed, instead exile it. Then remove it from the game. Then put it into the absolutely-removed-from-the-freaking-game-forever zone (treat it as though it doesn't exist).
