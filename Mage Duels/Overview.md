This is another WIP idea I have - a TCG

The idea is to make a game that really feels like an arena fight between two wizards.

'Higher Level Wizards' would have more prepared spells and a larger spellbook.

Speaking in Magic analogs:

#### Hand
Prepared spells. 

You start the game with 3 (depending on the power level), but you can take a game action to refill/switch out from your spellbook.

#### Library
Spellbook / Handybag (I might make them separate). The spellbook would have 12ish spells, and the handybag would have magic items (healing potion, sword). 

You could take an action to essentially Preordain (Look at the top 2 / Draw one of them or a random other card). 

Some class/ability might add the option to draw from anywhere in your spellbook. I can see how that might invite marked cards though.


#### Turn / APNAP system
Each player takes turns taking 2 actions, with a lore 'casting time' of 2s that is not relevant to the actual gameplay. While a spell is being cast, it may be countered by another spell of the same power but opposite element (or a null spell).
